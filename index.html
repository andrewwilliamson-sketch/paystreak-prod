Fair questions

What a careful operator asks first.

What's the catch?

There's no upfront cost. Paystreak earns an introduction fee only when a deal closes. Not every dataset finds a buyer, and some records can't be sold at all. We'll tell you early which applies to you.

Is my company a fit?

The strongest fits have 30 to 200 employees, run on modern software tools, and have well-defined workflows that people execute repeatedly, with a real body of internal documentation behind them. Technology companies, BPOs, recruiting firms, financial services, consulting, professional services, legal and compliance, healthcare administration, and logistics businesses are all common examples. If you're close but not exact, ask anyway.

What about client confidentiality?

Sensitive and confidential information is scrubbed before any downstream use, no customer information is exposed, and you approve every sample before it goes out. Privileged legal communications, patient records, and similar material need consent and legal review, and are often off the table entirely. We recommend having your own lawyer review any contract before you sign it.

Who are the buyers, and what do they do with the data?

Vetted programs that train and evaluate AI systems on realistic enterprise settings. They aren't trying to replicate your business, products, customers, or competitive advantages. Each buyer is vetted before we introduce them, and permitted uses are written into the license you approve.

What determines how much my data is worth?

Five things: the size and volume of the dataset, the complexity of the workflows it captures, how unique your operational knowledge is, the quality of the data, and the domain expertise it represents. Your free assessment covers each one.

How does Paystreak get paid?

We earn an introduction fee when a deal closes. There's nothing to pay upfront, no retainer, and nothing at all if a deal doesn't close.
