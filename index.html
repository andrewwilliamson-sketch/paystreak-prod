Your clients, your data, your terms

How your business stays protected.

  • Personal information comes out first.

    Names, contact details, and anything that identifies a person are redacted before anyone outside your business sees anything.

  • You keep 100% of your IP.

    Buyers license the data for agreed uses. They never own your records.

  • Non-exclusive by default.

    You're free to license the same dataset to more than one buyer, unless you choose an exclusive deal for a higher price.

  • We never take custody.

    Your data never touches our servers. It goes directly from you to the buyer.

  • No upfront cost.

    You pay nothing for the assessment or the search for buyers. Our introduction fee applies only if a deal closes.

  • Some data isn't a fit.

    Records covered by professional confidentiality or health privacy rules, such as privileged legal communications or patient records, need client consent and legal review. Often they can't be sold at all. If something can't or shouldn't be sold, we'll tell you plainly.

Fair questions

What a careful owner asks first.

What's the catch?

There's no upfront cost. Paystreak earns an introduction fee only when a deal closes. Not every dataset finds a buyer, and some records can't be sold at all. We'll tell you early which applies to you.

What about client confidentiality?

Personal information is redacted before any buyer sees anything, and you approve every sample before it goes out. Privileged legal communications, patient records, and similar material need client consent and legal review, and are often off the table entirely. We recommend having your own lawyer review any contract before you sign it.

Who are the buyers, and what do they do with the data?

Data-prep companies. They clean, label, and package data into training sets for companies that build AI systems. Each buyer is vetted before we introduce them, and permitted uses are written into the license you approve.

How does Paystreak get paid?

We earn an introduction fee when a deal closes. There's nothing to pay upfront, no retainer, and nothing at all if a deal doesn't close.
